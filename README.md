# 🚏 SheVibes — Rider's Stop

> **Where every ride counts.**

A women-focused route and travel-support web application designed to help users make more informed journey decisions by considering **safety, accessibility, transport availability, lighting, and community-reported conditions** — not just time, distance, and cost.

---

## 🌸 About the Project

Most navigation platforms focus primarily on three things:

* ⏱️ Time
* 📍 Distance
* 💰 Cost

But for many women travelling to **college, work, events, or home**, choosing a route can depend on much more.

A busy road during the afternoon can feel completely different after dark. Factors such as **street lighting, nearby public transport, accessibility, open spaces, and recent community reports** can influence how comfortable and suitable a journey feels.

**SheVibes — Rider's Stop** aims to provide a more user-focused approach to route decision-making by bringing these factors into consideration.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

> Most navigation platforms focus on three things: time, distance and cost. But for many women travelling to college, work, an event or home, the choice of route can depend on much more. A busy road in the afternoon may feel completely different after dark. Street lighting, nearby transport, accessibility, open spaces and recent community reports can all change the journey.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SheVibes — Rider's Stop is designed as a **women-centric travel and route-support platform** that goes beyond conventional navigation.

Instead of looking only at the fastest or shortest route, the platform aims to help users consider additional journey factors such as:

* 🛣️ Route conditions
* 💡 Street lighting
* 🚌 Nearby transportation
* ♿ Accessibility
* 🏙️ Surrounding environment
* 📢 Community reports
* 🌙 Day/night travel considerations

The goal is to help users make **more informed travel decisions** based on the context of their journey.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗺️ Route-Oriented Experience

Provides a journey-focused interface where users can explore travel information.

### 👩 Women-Centric Design

The platform is designed around travel considerations that may be particularly relevant to women.

### 💡 Environmental Awareness

Considers factors such as lighting and surrounding areas that may affect the travel experience.

### 🚌 Transport Awareness

Highlights the importance of nearby transportation options when planning a journey.

### 📢 Community Reports

Allows travel decisions to incorporate information reported by the community.

### 🌙 Context-Aware Travel

Recognises that the same route can have different conditions depending on the time of day.

### ♿ Accessibility Considerations

Considers accessibility as an additional factor in travel planning.
